# VIDEO PRODUCTION NOTES

## Recommended Visuals:

1. **Screen recordings** - Use actual app with sample data
2. **Smooth transitions** - Fade between sections
3. **Highlight circles/arrows** - Point to important features
4. **Split screen** - Show phone and WhatsApp side by side for messaging demo

## Audio Notes:

1. **Background music** - Upbeat but not distracting
2. **Narrator voice** - Friendly, conversational, confident
3. **Sound effects** - Subtle clicks for button taps

## Timing Breakdown:

| Section | Duration | Cumulative |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Intro | 30 sec | 0:30 |
| Login & Dashboard | 30 sec | 1:00 |
| Creating Sale | 60 sec | 2:00 |
| Reminders | 45 sec | 2:45 |
| Customer Warranty | 30 sec | 3:15 |
| Inventory | 30 sec | 3:45 |
| Money & Expenses | 30 sec | 4:15 |
| WhatsApp Summary | 15 sec | 4:30 |
| Closing | 30 sec | 5:00 |

## Sample Data to Prepare:

1. **Customers**: Sharma ji, Gupta family, Patel Electronics
2. **Products**: RO Water Purifier, Carbon Filter, RO Membrane, UV Chamber
3. **Reminders**: Mix of overdue, today, upcoming across customers
4. **Transactions**: Week's worth of sales and expenses
5. **Low stock items**: 2-3 items below minimum threshold

## Key Messages to Emphasize:

1. "Never miss a service call" - repeat 2-3 times
2. "100% maintenance revenue" - the core value prop
3. "One tap" - emphasize simplicity
4. "Professional" - for customer communication
5. "3-4 months" - when recommendations kick in
