# Kuberbook Demo Video: Production Instructions

This document outlines the step-by-step flow for the Kuberbook product demo video. 

## Scene 1: Inventory Management
**Objective**: Show how easy it is to set up a digital shop.

1.  **Action**: Navigate to the **Inventory** tab.
2.  **Action**: Click on **"Add Item"** (or the plus icon).
3.  **Details**: Enter a sample item (e.g., "Aqua Guard RO").
4.  **Details**: Set the stock quantity and price.
5.  **Visual**: Ensure the item appears in the list after saving.

---

## Scene 2: Creating a Sale (The Full Workflow)
**Objective**: Showcase the powerful single-screen sales interface.

1.  **Action**: Go to the **New Sale** or **Dashboard** sales section.
2.  **Customer Info**: 
    - Click **"Add New Customer"**.
    - Enter a Name and Phone Number. (use your number)
3.  **Item Selection**: 
    - Search for and select the item you just added to the inventory.
4.  **Advanced Details**:
    - **Warranty**: Add a warranty period (e.g., 12 months).
    - **Servicing**: Add recurring servicing (e.g., Every 3 months).
5.  **Pricing**: 
    - Apply a small **Discount** (e.g., Flat ₹100 or 10%).
6.  **Action**: Click **"Complete Sale"** or **"Generate Bill"**.

---

## Scene 3: Billing & Digital Delivery
**Objective**: Demonstrate the digital-first professional billing.

1.  **Visual**: The generated bill/invoice pops up on the screen (the screenshot/bill view).
2.  **WhatsApp Action**:
    - Click the **"Send on WhatsApp"** button.
    - Show the transition to WhatsApp.
    - Send the message containing the bill link.
3.  **Link Interaction**:
    - Open the **Bill Link** as if you are the customer.
    - Show the sleek, mobile-responsive bill page.

---

## Scene 4: Payments Integration
**Objective**: Show how easy it is for customers to pay.

1.  **Action**: On the digital bill page, click the **"Pay via Google Pay"** (or UPI) button.
2.  **Visual**: Show the Google Pay interface/intent opening.
3.  **Context**: Demonstrate that it works and is ready for payment.

---

## Scene 5: Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
**Objective**: Show the backend organization.

1.  **Action**: Navigate back to the dashboard and open the **Customers** tab.
2.  **Visual**: Show that the customer we just added is now listed with their full details.
3.  **Detail**: Verify all data (phone, previous purchase) is correctly logged.

---

## Scene 6: Automated Reminders
**Objective**: Show the "recurring revenue/maintenance" power of the app.

1.  **Action**: Navigate to the **Reminders** tab.
2.  **View**: 
    - Show the **"Today's Tasks"** or **"Ending Today"** section.
3.  **Action**: Click the **"Send Message"** button next to a reminder.
4.  **WhatsApp Action**: Show the message being sent to the customer in one go.

---

### General Video Guidelines:
- **Pacing**: Keep actions smooth but fast enough to maintain interest.
- **Visuals**: Use a clear, high-resolution screen recording.
- **Privacy**: Use demo/fake data for numbers and names.
- **Tone**: Professional, efficient, and modern.
